# Maltiva POS

## Register

product

## Users

Cashiers at a takeaway counter, managers reviewing stock and reports, and owners administering the store. The documented hardware is a 1280×720 touch display on an older Windows PC with a thermal printer.

## Product Purpose

Enter orders quickly, collect payment, issue tokens and receipts, and retain reliable sales and stock records through interruptions. Prices and financial records are authoritative on the server.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, calm. Preserve the existing Maltiva identity and established POS controls.

## Anti-references

The specification references Square, Toast and Loyverse for workflow rather than visual copying. Avoid decorative blur, large shadows and long animations on the target hardware.

## Design Principles

- Keep checkout fast and understandable during counter service.
- Protect saved sales and recoverable drafts before convenience.
- Explain failures with a concrete recovery action.
- Preserve established controls and role boundaries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Follow the existing specification's high contrast, touch-friendly controls and short motion. Provide visible labels, keyboard focus, accessible errors and reduced-motion support. This context records the existing specification and implementation rather than introducing a new visual direction.
